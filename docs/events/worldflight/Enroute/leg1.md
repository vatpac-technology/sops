## Sector Responsibilities
### Gundagai (GUN)
GUN will receive a single stream of traffic via KADOM. Ensure sufficient vertical separation exists between any aircraft operating in close proximity.

!!! note
    Due to the long sector length, it is likely that overtaking will take place during the event. Aircraft **should not** be issued with speed instructions unless required to maintain separation assurance.

### Armidale (ARL)
ARL will receive a single stream of traffic from the SY TMA tracking `UNTAV MDG ENPAG` or assigned an equivalent vector. These aircraft should be vectored until clear of the GUN sector, then cleared direct to **ENPAG** if necessary. Ensure sufficient vertical separation exists between any aircraft operating in close proximity.

No coordination is required with ASP(BKE) prior to clearing an aircraft direct to ENPAG.

!!! note
    The official route for departures is via KADOM and all aircraft departing from the eastern runway will be provided with an amended route (`UNTAV MDG ENPAG`) by SY ACD. It is preferred that pilots track this route under their own navigation but ARL (and surrounding controllers) should be ready to provide vectors to aircraft who are unable to track via the new waypoints.

### Bourke (BKE)
ASP will receive two streams of traffic, one from GUN and one from ARL. Both will be merging at **ENPAG**.

Ensure adequate vertical separation exists between aircraft operating in close proximity and pay particular attention to aircraft approaching the merge point.

!!! note
    Due to the long sector length, it is likely that overtaking will take place during the event. Aircraft **should not** be issued with speed instructions unless required to maintain separation assurance.

### Mt Isa (ISA)
ISA will receive one stream of traffic from ASP. Ensure adequate vertical separation exists between aircraft operating in close proximity.

!!! note
    Due to the long sector length, it is likely that overtaking will take place during the event. Aircraft **should not** be issued with speed instructions unless required to maintain separation assurance.

!!! tip
    Frequency congestion may be reduced by having additional controllers open WEG and/or STR.

### Territory (TRT)
TRT will receive one stream of traffic from ISA. Ensure adequate vertical separation exists between aircraft operating in close proximity.

!!! note
    Due to the long sector length, it is likely that overtaking will take place during the event. Aircraft **should not** be issued with speed instructions unless required to maintain separation assurance.

Event aircraft shall be transferred to WAAF CTR approaching the FIR boundary.

### All Other Sectors
Due to the sheer volume of traffic departing from YSSY, arrivals will **not be possible** during the departure event. Any aircraft inbound to YSSY should be strongly encouraged to divert to another aerodrome.

## Coordination
### SY TCU to ARL
Voiceless for all aircraft:

- Assigned the lower of `F280` or the `RFL`; and  
- Tracking via `UNTAV MDG` or assigned an equivalent vector

All other aircraft going to ARL CTA will be **Heads-up** Coordinated.

### TRT to WAAF
As per [standard TRT coordination procedures](../../../../../enroute/brisbane/TRT/#international-waaf), all aircraft should be **Heads-up Coordinated** to WAAF CTR prior to **30 mins** from boundary.

!!! tip
    It may be prudent (especially if they are using vatSys) to set up a Voiceless Coordination Agreement with WAAF CTR, in order to reduce workload for both controllers.
