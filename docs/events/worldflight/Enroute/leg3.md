## Temporary Positions
### Karratha (YPKA)
For the duration of the event, YPKA will be reclassified as a Class C Radar Tower.

Two temporary surveillance TCU positions are responsible for the airspace within 31 DME from `SFC` to `F185`. **KA APP** is responsible for the airspace north of the extended runway centreline, **KA DEP** is responsible for the airspace to the south.

#### Airspace Reclassification
All Class D & E airspace within 31 DME is reclassified as Class C, extending from their respective bases to `F245`.

### Alice Springs (YBAS)
For the duration of the event, YBAS will be reclassified as a Class C Radar Tower.

All existing Class D airspace is reclassified as Class C and two temporary surveillance TCU units are responsible for the airspace within 36 DME (39 DME to the southeast) from `SFC` to `F185`. 

#### Airspace Division
=== "Runway 12"
    The TMA is split perpendicular to the extended runway centreline. **AS APP** is responsible for the airspace to the west, **AS DEP** is responsible for the airspace to the east.

=== "Runway 30"
    The TMA is split along the extended runway centreline. **AS APP** is responsible for the airspace to the south, **AS DEP** is responsible for the airspace to the north.

## Sector Responsibilities
### Port (POT)
The airspace immediately east of the KA TMA shall be [released to POT](#pot-airspace-release) from OLW. All event aircraft *departing* YPKA will be transferred directly from KA TCU to POT.

The total sector length is approximately 1,000nm. If large gaps exist in the conga line, aircraft may be allowed to overtake each other. If the departure sequence is tight, speed control should be used to prevent overtaking.

Ensure adequate vertical separation exists between any aircraft operating in close proximity.

### Newman (NEW)
The total sector length is approximately 1,000nm. If large gaps exist in the conga line, aircraft may be allowed to overtake each other. If the departure sequence is tight, speed control should be used to prevent overtaking.

Ensure adequate vertical separation exists between any aircraft operating in close proximity.

### Kimberley (KIY)
KIY shall perform initial sequencing actions for aircraft inbound to YBAS, through the use of speed control.

When transferring aircraft to ASW, KIY shall instruct event aircraft to expect their arrival details for YBAS.

!!! phraseology
    **KIY**: "QFA26, contact Melbourne Centre 131.8, expect arrival details" 

### Alice Springs West (ASW)
ASW is responsible for issuing arrival instructions and final sequencing actions in to YBAS.

When **ASP** is online, the airspace immediately west of the AS TMA shall be [released to ASW](#asw-airspace-release). All event aircraft inbound to YBAS will be transferred directly from ASW to AS TCU.

#### Arrival Procedures
There are no STARs into YBAS. All event aircraft shall be recleared via `LISZT` (for ILS runway 12) or `AS2EC` (for RNP-Z runway 30) by ASW.

KIY will transfer aircraft to ASW expecting arrival detials. 

!!! phraseology
    **QFA26**: "Melbourne Centre, QFA26, maintaining FL370"  
    **ASW**: "QFA26, Melbourne Centre, cleared direct Alice Springs Echo Charlie, when ready descend to `F130`"

#### Sequencing
KIY will perform initial sequencing actions for event aircraft. ASW shall perform any final sequencing actions.

Minimum distance between arrivals at handoff to AS TCU is **10nm** (with no significant closing speed). It is important to try not to exceed **15nm**, due to the large number of arrivals.

## Coordination
### ASW to AS TCU
Voiceless for all aircraft:

- Assigned `A090`; and
- Tracking via either `LISZT` or `AS2EC`

All other aircraft going to AS TCU CTA will be **Heads-up** Coordinated.

### ASW Airspace Release
When ASP is online, the airspace immediately west of the AS TMA is released to ASW to facilitate YBAS arrivals.

### POT Airspace Release
When OLW is online, the airspace immediately east of the KA TMA is released to POT to facilitate YPKA departures to YBAS.
