## Airspace Division
Standard airspace divisions will be used for the event. The airspace shall be divided between two Departures controllers, as per below:

| Departures South (SDS) | Departures North (SDN) |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- |
| SDS                    | SDN                    |
| SAS                    | SAN                    |
| SWA                    | SRA                    |

!!! note
    Only one Departures controller will be required in the event of a single runway mode at YSSY.

## Departure Procedures
Aircraft departing from runways 16R/34L will be assigned the procedural SID terminating at KADOM.

Aircraft departing from runways 16L/34R will be assigned the **SY (RADAR)** SID with the [standard assignable heading](../../../aerodromes/classc/sydney.md#standard-assignable-departure-headings). These aircraft shall be vectored to the north of YSSY and cleared direct to `UNTAV`. If the pilot is unable to accept tracking as per the [amended route](../Aerodromes/sydneydep.md#amended-routes-from-eastern-aprons), they should be assigned an equivalent vector.

## Coordination
### SY ADC
#### Auto Release
Standard as per [YSSY Local Instructions](../../../../../aerodromes/classc/sydney/#auto-release).

### ARL
Voiceless for all aircraft:

- Assigned the lower of `F280` or the `RFL`; and  
- Tracking via `UNTAV MDG` or assigned an equivalent vector

All other aircraft going to ARL CTA will be **Heads-up** Coordinated.

!!! tip
    Ensure headings are [annotated appropriately](../../../../../client/annotations/#route-and-positions) in the aircraft's label data field and the aircraft's route is up to date, to provide situational awareness to receiving controllers.
